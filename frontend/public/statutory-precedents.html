Precedents & Statutory Provisions

Direct case precedents from the Indian Patent Office (IPO), Intellectual Property Appellate Board (IPAB), and international patent offices concerning Ayurvedic compositions.

Patents Act § 3(p)
Traditional Knowledge Bar

Bars patenting an invention that in effect is traditional knowledge or an aggregation/duplication of known properties of traditionally known components.

Patents Act § 3(e)
Mere Admixture Restriction

Substances formed by mere admixture resulting only in aggregation of properties are non-patentable unless an unexpected synergistic effect is established.

Patents Rules, Rule 13(7)
Biological Resource Disclosure

Mandatory statutory declaration specifying the source and geographical origin of biological materials procured within India.

Controller of Patents vs. Polyherbal Classical Formulation

Revoked § 3(p)
Application No: 2018/DEL/10429 Prior Art Index: TKDL Ref #AY-402

"The applicant claimed an improved therapeutic process for an herbal mixture derived from Chyawanprash. The Assistant Controller ruled that minor modification of extraction steps or common carrier addition does not overcome the Section 3(p) statutory bar unless a demonstrable synergistic enhancement beyond textbook properties is established."

Statutory Takeaway: Formulations matching First Schedule authoritative texts are conclusively traditional knowledge.

Curcumin & Piperine Bioavailability Synergy Exception

Allowed § 3(e)
Patent Grant: IN-284910 Jurisdiction: IPO New Delhi

"The patent was allowed after the applicant provided verified pharmacokinetics confirming a 2000% increase in active serum bioavailability when combining piperine with curcumin at precise ratios, proving a clear non-obvious synergistic effect over single-constituent baselines."

Statutory Takeaway: Synergistic indices (CI < 1) and clinical comparative data overcome Section 3(e) objections.

CSIR vs. USPTO / EPO (Turmeric & Neem Prior-Art Revocation)

TKDL Defensive Precedent
Patent References: US 5,401,504 / EP 0436257 Challenger: CSIR / Government of India

"Patents granted on the wound-healing properties of turmeric and the antifungal properties of neem were revoked internationally after the CSIR submitted documented ancient Sanskrit texts and TKDL entries confirming these properties were long-standing Indian traditional knowledge."

Statutory Takeaway: Unmodified single-herb traditional utilities cannot satisfy novelty requirements globally.

Polyherbal Respiratory Admixture Rejection

Refused § 3(e)
Application No: 2020/MUM/08912 Target: Adhatoda vasica + Ocimum sanctum extract

"The Controller ruled that combining hydro-alcoholic extracts of Vasa and Tulsi without demonstrating a novel mechanism of action or superior potency represents a mere aggregation of known therapeutic properties."

Statutory Takeaway: Polyherbal compositions require quantitative evidence that the combination exceeds the sum of its individual components.
