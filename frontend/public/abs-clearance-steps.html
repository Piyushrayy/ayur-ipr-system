National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) Clearance

Biological Diversity Act (2024 Amendment) ABS Regulatory Flow

Compliance Checklist & Timeline
Mandatory Statutory Bar: Under Section 6 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 (amended 2024), any applicant seeking patent protection inside or outside India for an invention based on biological resources or associated knowledge obtained from India must obtain prior approval from the National Biodiversity Authority.
1

Entity Jurisdiction Assessment (Section 3 vs. Section 7)

Timeline: Day 1–10

Establish legal status to determine the appropriate statutory pathway:

Section 7 (Domestic / Indian Entity)

Indian citizens and registered domestic entities require prior intimation to the concerned State Biodiversity Board (SBB).

Section 3 (Foreign / Non-Resident)

Entities with non-resident Indian equity, foreign incorporation, or overseas collaboration require formal approval from the NBA.

2

Form III Statutory Filing (IPR Clearance Application)

Filing Window: Before Patent Grant

File Form III on the centralized PARIVESH / NBA portal for permission to apply for a patent right:

  • Submit official Indian Patent Application Number and complete patent claims.
  • Disclose geographical origin (district, state, and forest division) of every herb used.
  • Submit statutory filing fee of ₹500 as prescribed by NBA regulations.
3

Access & Benefit Sharing (ABS) Agreement Execution

Timeline: 60–90 Days

Execute a legally binding agreement committing monetary and non-monetary benefit-sharing to local communities:

• Gross Ex-factory Sales Tier: 0.1% to 0.5% of annual sales depending on production scale.
• Royalty on Licensing: 2.0% to 5.0% of the total license fee paid to the National Biodiversity Fund.
4

Ayush Practitioner & Cultivated Herbs Exemptions (2024 Rules)

Statutory Relief

The 2024 amendments provide specific exemptions from benefit-sharing obligations:

  • Registered AYUSH Practitioners: Traditional Vaidyas, Hakims, and local healers practicing non-commercial dispensing are exempt.
  • Cultivated Medicinal Plants: Exemption from access approval when raw materials are sourced from documented cultivated agricultural lands.
